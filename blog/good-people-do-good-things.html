Good People Do Good Things

"Good People Do Good Things" is an inspiring initiative undertaken by 'withit.' to make a positive impact on social causes that deeply resonate with the brand. Building a fashion brand with a strong commitment to giving back is not just a strategic choice for 'withit.', but a critical foundation rooted in the values and experiences of its Founder, Faisal Tayan. From a young age, Faisal has been actively involved in various charitable endeavors, instilling in him a deep sense of empathy and compassion. It is this ingrained belief that motivates 'withit.' to create products that not only embody exceptional quality and style but also serve a greater purpose.

Faisal Tayan firmly believes that true success lies in creating something that genuinely helps others while showcasing his skills, talents, and creativity. For him, the purpose of 'withit.' extends beyond the realm of fashion; it is about making a meaningful difference in the world. Faisal recognizes that playing small and simply existing does not serve anyone, and thus, he embraces the quote, "Playing small does not serve the world," as a personal mantra.

With the "Good People Do Good Things" initiative, 'withit.' strives to make the world a better place by actively engaging in social causes. Through their collaborations, donations, and conscious efforts, they aim to uplift communities, amplify important voices, and inspire positive change. Faisal Tayan's vision for 'withit.' goes beyond personal success; it is about harnessing the platform and influence of the brand to create a ripple effect of goodness and impact.

By integrating the belief that good people do good things into the core of their operations, 'withit.' sets an example for the fashion industry and beyond. They demonstrate that businesses have the power and responsibility to make a difference, to help shape a better future, and to create a world where kindness and compassion thrive.

In essence, 'withit.' embodies the conviction that fashion can be a force for good, and that by aligning their talents, skills, and resources with social causes, they can help create a more inclusive, compassionate, and better world for all.
