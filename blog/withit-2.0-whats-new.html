Withit 2.0... Whats New

Manchester-based fashion brand is relaunching with a new look, new products, and a new approach to fashion. The brand is transitioning from a simple one-page website to a fully custom brand with high-end cut & sew products and a completely custom website. The brand's focus is on producing the finest custom high-end tracksuits, hoodies, joggers, caps, shorts, gym tops, and clothing. The brand is dedicated to sampling exciting materials to produce the best quality products, all from Manchester.

The brand has always been known for its simplicity and understated elegance, but the relaunch is taking things to the next level. The new custom website has been designed from the ground up to showcase the brand's new direction. The site features detailed product descriptions, high-quality images, and easy navigation to help customers find exactly what they're looking for.

One of the key highlights of the relaunch is the brand's new line of high-end cut & sew products. These products are made using the finest materials and are constructed with care and attention to detail. From the luxurious feel of the fabric to the precise stitching, every aspect of these products has been designed to create a truly premium experience. The brand is committed to providing customers with the best possible quality, and these new products are a testament to that commitment.

Another exciting aspect of the relaunch is the brand's focus on customization. Customers can now choose from a variety of materials, colors, and styles to create a unique piece of clothing that reflects their personal style. Whether it's a custom tracksuit or a personalized hoodie, the brand is dedicated to helping customers express their individuality through fashion.

The brand's commitment to Manchester is also evident in the relaunch. All products are designed and manufactured in Manchester, ensuring that the brand stays true to its roots. The brand is proud to be a part of the city's rich history of textile manufacturing and is committed to supporting the local economy by keeping production local.

In conclusion, the relaunch of this Manchester-based fashion brand marks a new chapter in the brand's history. The focus on high-end cut & sew products, customization, and local production sets the brand apart and demonstrates its commitment to quality and innovation. With the new custom website and exciting product line, the brand is poised to continue its growth and establish itself as a leader in the fashion industry.
